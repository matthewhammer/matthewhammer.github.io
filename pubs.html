Thesis

  • Self-Adjusting Machines Matthew A. Hammer Ph.D. dissertation, University of Chicago. December 2012. It describes the design and implementation of CEAL, a C-based language for self-adjusting computation: a family of language-based techniques for incremental computation, where programs that look conventional consume and produce data that changes over time.
