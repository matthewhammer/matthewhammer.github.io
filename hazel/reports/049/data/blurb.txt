The Witch slide's whole program, compiled to JavaScript by hazel-jit and run in the page at a slider's step instead of waiting for Hazel's worker. In Firefox it answers in 8 to 21 ms (2 to 3 ms for the next event of a drag), all 53 livelits' views included, with the same drawings on every polygon slide. On every step the picture changed at the page's first redraw, 286 to 386 ms after it, against 1.35 to 1.74 s with nothing compiled, once a step back to a slider's starting value stopped being taken for an undo. What is left is the page's own work around the run: statics, the whole slide's view drawn twice, and the program compiled again after each step. Try it yourself, with a frame-rate overlay. Also why the first numbers, in Node, measured a program the page never holds.
