The Witch slide's whole program, compiled to JavaScript by hazel-jit and run in the page at a slider's step instead of waiting for Hazel's worker. In Firefox it answers in 10 to 12 ms (3 to 4 ms for the next event of a drag), all 53 livelits' views included, with the same drawings on every polygon slide. In 2 of 4 steps the picture changed at the page's first redraw, 318 and 469 ms after the step, against 1.9 to 2.2 s with nothing compiled; on the steps back to a slider's starting value it still waited for the worker. What is left is the page's own work around the run: statics, the whole slide's view drawn twice, and the program compiled again after each step. Also why the first numbers, in Node, measured a program the page never holds.
