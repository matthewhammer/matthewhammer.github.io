Can a livelit's slider in Hazel move at 60 frames a second? On the Witch slide, a drag now runs at 48.4 fps with views and the slide's whole run compiled to JavaScript by hazel-jit, the picture following at every frame and the median frame one display frame (17.1 ms); a step shows in 176 to 290 ms, down from 4.2 to 6.5 s. Eight numbered builds of one branch, each measured the same way in Firefox, show how it got there; compiled code runs 29 to 1,140 times faster than the interpreter, and the page's own view is most of what is left. Reports 045 to 049 together, for the meeting with Cyrus, with screenshots and a frame-rate overlay to try it yourself.
